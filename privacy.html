Privacy Policy

KASA – Sales & Finance Tracker

UK GDPR Compliant

This Privacy Policy explains how KASA ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your personal data. We are committed to complying with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Last updated: June 2026

1. Who We Are

KASA is operated by Anil Ataman, based in London, United Kingdom.
Contact: anilatamans@gmail.com

2. What Data We Collect

We collect only the data you provide directly:

  • Account data: Email address and password (used to create and access your account)
  • Business data: Business name, owner name (optional, stored in your account profile)
  • Financial data: Sales records, income, expenses, and product information you enter into the App
  • Usage data: Basic app interaction data to maintain functionality (e.g. last sync time)

We do not collect payment card details, government IDs, or any sensitive personal data beyond the above.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and operate the KASA service
  • To sync your data securely across your devices
  • To generate reports and insights within the App
  • To respond to your support requests

Our lawful basis for processing is contract performance (providing the service you signed up for) and legitimate interests (maintaining security and improving the App).

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (supabase.com), a cloud database provider. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Each user's data is isolated using Row Level Security (RLS) — no other user can access your records.

Supabase stores data in data centres within the European Economic Area (EEA). Transfers outside the UK/EEA, if any, are covered by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) in accordance with UK GDPR Article 46.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account or request data deletion, your data will be permanently removed within 30 days.

6. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectify any inaccurate data
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict processing of your data
  • Data portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at anilatamans@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk

7. Subscriptions & Payments

KASA offers a free trial followed by an auto-renewable subscription. Subscription payments are processed exclusively through Apple's App Store. We do not directly process or store any payment card information. Apple's privacy policy applies to payment transactions.

Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time through your Apple ID account settings. Under the UK Consumer Rights Act 2015, you have the right to cancel within 14 days of purchase (cooling-off period), unless you have already begun using the service.

8. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — database and authentication (privacy policy)
  • Apple App Store — app distribution and payment processing (privacy policy)

We do not use advertising networks, analytics trackers, or sell your data to any third party.

9. Children's Privacy

KASA is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
